# Paragraphs, spacing and lists

Paragraph settings affect the paragraphs containing the selection, or the paragraph at the caret. Open an ordinary document and expand **Paragraph**.

## Steps

1. Choose **Align left**, **Center**, **Align right** or **Justify**.
2. **First-line indent** sets the first-line indent. **Increase line spacing** and **Decrease line spacing** adjust line spacing in steps.
3. In **Lists**, choose a bulleted or numbered list. Clearing the list returns its items to ordinary paragraphs.

## Check, undo and limitations

Check several lines and neighbouring paragraphs: alignment affects more than an individual word. Enter breaks a line without starting another paragraph; Ctrl+Enter creates a paragraph or the next list item. Use Ctrl+Z to undo. If formatting disappears after reopening, use RTF/XamlPackage rather than TXT/XAML. Markdown lists are edited in their source text.
